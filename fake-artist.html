Rules & Setup

The Premise

Real artists share a secret word and must draw it cooperatively. The Fake Artist knows only the category and must blend in without getting exposed.

Player Count & Imposter Ratios

Works best for 5 to 10 players. Suggested imposter counts:

Players Recommended Game Dynamic
4 – 6 1 Imposter Fast, tight social deduction
7 – 9 1 or 2 Imposters 2 creates paranoia & chaos
10 – 12 2 Imposters Ideal for large party gatherings
13+ 2 or 3 Imposters Epic high-player showdown

Drawing Rules

• Each player gets a different color pen.
• In clockwise order, draw one continuous line (no lifting the pen).
• Complete 2 rounds total so each player draws two lines.
• Vote simultaneously on the Fake Artist!

Winning Conditions

• If the Fake Artist is NOT caught, the Fake Artist wins!
• If the Fake Artist IS caught, they can still win if they correctly name the secret word!
