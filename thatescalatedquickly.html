How To Play

1

Secret Numbers (1 to 10)

One player acts as the Captain (guesser) for the round. Every other player secretly receives a private number from 1 to 10.

2

Draw a Card

Draw a question. 1 represents the most chill/mild answer, while 10 represents absolute, unhinged escalation!

3

Improvise Answers

Each player improvises a response corresponding to the exact intensity of their secret number. Need inspiration? Tap the 💡 Hint bulb!

4

Rank & Reveal

The Captain must guess and arrange the players in ascending order from 1 to 10. Reveal your numbers to see if you survived the round!
